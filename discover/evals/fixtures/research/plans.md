## Q-001 What causes members to miss the load deadline for a firing

- **Status:** answered (→ E-001, E-002, E-005, E-008) — caveat: 5 entries against the 8 planned; sessions 6 and 7 added no new claims
- **Question:** Whether the studio should change how firing schedules reach members before it changes the booking flow
- **Method:** Interviews, 5–8 people — a missed deadline is a why question, and motivation only surfaces in open conversation
- **Participants:** Members of the two partner studios (Northside Clay, Wheelhouse Collective) who have fired at least one piece in the last two months; excludes staff and anyone who works in UX or market research; 6–8 members plus one manager walkthrough
- **Timeline:** 2 weeks of sessions, 3 days synthesis
- **Done when:** 8–12 entries appended to the evidence log with Question: Q-001, contradictions flagged, one recommendation drafted
- **Risks:** Members who already lapsed over missed firings are unreachable; both partner studios fire weekly, so monthly-firing studios may differ
- **Fielded:** 2026-07-14 to 2026-07-25 — 7 member sessions (P1–P7), guide v1 for P1–P3 and guide v2 for P4–P7, plus one manager walkthrough (P8)

## Q-002 Whether members can book a shelf in the next firing unaided in the new booking flow

- **Status:** open
- **Question:** Whether the booking flow ships to all studios in October or goes back for another round
- **Method:** Usability test, 5 people — 5 sessions find most usability problems; more adds little
- **Participants:** Members of the partner studios who have fired at least one piece in the last two months and have not seen the new flow; excludes staff and anyone in UX, design, or research; 5 members
- **Timeline:** 1 week of sessions, 2 days synthesis
- **Done when:** 5–8 entries appended to the evidence log with Question: Q-002, counts against pre-defined task criteria
- **Risks:** Partner-studio members know the old flow and may navigate on habit; a staging build without live kiln data cannot show a full firing

## Q-003 How common it is for members to check a piece's firing status by messaging the manager rather than in the app

- **Status:** open
- **Question:** Whether firing-status visibility earns a place on the autumn roadmap ahead of booking
- **Method:** Survey — only counts generalize; run after interviews, and E-007 and E-008 give the questions their language
- **Participants:** All current members of the two partner studios (about 180), target 60 completes; excludes staff
- **Timeline:** 3-day pilot, then 1 week fielding, 2 days analysis
- **Done when:** 3–5 entries appended to the evidence log with Question: Q-003, counts reported per questionnaire version, response rate logged
- **Risks:** Members active in the WhatsApp group are the likeliest to respond, which inflates the messaging count; a survey sent by the manager reads as a request from the manager

## Q-004 How studio managers decide what goes into a firing when more pieces are ready than the kiln holds

- **Status:** open
- **Question:** Whether Kilnwise should build firings from member bookings, or keep the manager's judgment as the source of truth
- **Method:** Interviews, 5–8 people — the decision happens in the manager's head on a full morning; only open conversation gets at it
- **Participants:** Managers or firing technicians who load a shared kiln at least twice a month at studios with 30 or more members; excludes the partner studios' manager already interviewed (P8) and anyone in UX or market research; 6 managers, recruited from the regional ceramics guild list
- **Timeline:** 3 weeks of sessions (managers are hard to schedule), 3 days synthesis
- **Done when:** 6–10 entries appended to the evidence log with Question: Q-004, contradictions flagged
- **Risks:** The guild list skews toward well-run studios; a manager may describe the policy rather than what happens on a full morning

## Q-005 How the tools studios compare Kilnwise to handle firing schedules, load deadlines, and shelf booking

- **Status:** open
- **Question:** Whether the October roadmap needs a firing-schedule feature to hold its own against alternatives, or whether booking alone is the gap
- **Method:** Competitive teardown — faster than asking members to describe alternatives from memory
- **Participants:** Not applicable — two named competitors, Bisqueline and Firelane, both raised unprompted by P2 and P6 during Q-001
- **Timeline:** 2 days
- **Done when:** One teardown file per competitor plus the comparison, and 4–6 entries appended to the evidence log with Question: Q-005, with inferences about member demand capped at low
- **Risks:** Public pages show positioning, not the product; convergence between competitors is not evidence of demand

## Q-006 What members of other studios complain about in the firing-schedule tools they already use

- **Status:** open
- **Question:** Whether the missed-firing problem in Q-001 is specific to the partner studios or general to the category
- **Method:** Review mining, 2+ source types — real member language from studios we cannot reach; self-selected and secondhand, so findings cap at low
- **Participants:** Proxy sources in the plan's order of strength: no support archive exists for other studios; public app-store reviews of Firelane and Bisqueline; the Clayboard community forum's scheduling threads
- **Timeline:** 2 days
- **Done when:** A mining file for Q-006 and 3–6 entries appended to the evidence log at low confidence with Question: Q-006, each naming the firsthand method that would confirm it
- **Risks:** Reviewers are the angriest and happiest members, never the median; a complaint about one competitor's app may be about that app, not the category
