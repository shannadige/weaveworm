## E-001 Members learn a firing's load deadline from the studio whiteboard, not from the app
- **Evidence:** 6 of 7 member interviewees named the whiteboard by the kiln room as where they check; P2: "I look at the board when I walk in. I couldn't tell you where it is in the app." The manager walkthrough (P8) confirmed the whiteboard is the only posted schedule at either studio.
- **Source:** interviews 2026-07 (P1–P7), guide v1 and v2; manager walkthrough 2026-07-25 (P8)
- **Question:** Q-001
- **Confidence:** high — 6 of 7 members, and the walkthrough observed the board as the sole posted schedule

## E-002 Members miss the load deadline because they never see the app's firing notification
- **Evidence:** P1, P3, and P5 said notifications were switched off or went unnoticed; P3: "It's in with forty other things on my lock screen. I don't see it."
- **Source:** interviews 2026-07 (P1–P7), guide v1
- **Question:** Q-001
- **Confidence:** medium — self-reported by three participants
- **Stakes:** high — a piece that misses a bisque firing waits two weeks; two members lapsed in spring citing missed firings

## E-003 Members who share a shelf with another member rarely miss a load deadline
- **Evidence:** P4 and P6 each described a shelfmate who texts them before a firing; P6: "She loads on Thursdays, so she pings me Wednesday night. I've never missed one since."
- **Source:** interviews 2026-07 (P1–P7), guide v2
- **Question:** Q-001
- **Confidence:** medium — self-reported by two participants

## E-005 Members see the app's firing notification, but it arrives after the load deadline has passed
- **Evidence:** P6 and P7 opened their notification history in session: both showed firing alerts timestamped 40 minutes and 2 hours after the kiln was loaded. P7: "By the time it buzzes, the door's shut." The manager (P8) confirmed the schedule is entered after loading.
- **Source:** interviews 2026-07 (P1–P7), guide v2; manager walkthrough 2026-07-25 (P8)
- **Question:** Q-001
- **Confidence:** high — observed notification timestamps from two participants, confirmed by the manager
- **Contradicts:** E-002

## E-006 Studio managers enter the firing schedule into Kilnwise only after the kiln is loaded
- **Evidence:** The manager walkthrough (P8) showed the last 6 firings entered 35 minutes to 19 hours after each load; P8: "I write it on the board when I load, and put it in the app when I'm back at the desk."
- **Source:** manager walkthrough 2026-07-25 (P8), Kilnwise schedule timestamps 2026-06-10 to 2026-07-22
- **Question:** unplanned (manager-scheduling)
- **Confidence:** high — observed timestamps across 6 firings

## E-007 Members ask whether a piece has been fired in the studio WhatsApp group rather than in the app
- **Evidence:** 23 of 61 messages in the group export are firing-status questions from 9 distinct members (M1–M9); M4: "did the mugs on the top shelf go in last night or not??"
- **Source:** studio WhatsApp group export 2026-06 (61 messages, M1–M9), supplied by the manager
- **Question:** unplanned (firing-status-check)
- **Confidence:** low — proxy source, one studio, one month

## E-008 Members cannot tell from the app whether their piece has been fired
- **Evidence:** P2, P5, and P7 each described walking to the studio or messaging the manager to find out; P5: "The app says 'booked'. It said 'booked' before the firing and it says 'booked' after."
- **Source:** interviews 2026-07 (P1–P7), guide v2
- **Question:** Q-001
- **Confidence:** medium — self-reported by three participants
