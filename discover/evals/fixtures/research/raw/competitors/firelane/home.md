# Firelane

## Never miss a firing again.
Firelane tells your members when the kiln is loading, what's going in, and when their work is ready to collect.

Built for shared kilns: community studios, university ceramics departments, and makerspaces.

### For members
- Get a text 48 hours before a firing is loaded
- Reserve a spot on the shelf for the next bisque or glaze firing
- See when your pieces are fired and ready to collect

### For kiln managers
- Publish firings from your phone in under a minute
- See who has reserved space and what's still open
- Firing log with cone, temperature, and load photos

Trusted by 140 studios. From $79 a month.
