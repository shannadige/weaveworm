# Publishing a firing

1. From the Kilns tab, choose the kiln and tap New firing.
2. Set the type (bisque or glaze), the load date and time, and how many shelves are open for reservation.
3. Tap Publish. Members receive an email immediately; on the Guild plan they also receive a text 48 hours before the load time, and a second text 2 hours before.
4. On load day, mark the firing as Loading. Members who reserved a spot but whose work is not on the shelf are notified that their reservation lapsed.
5. When the kiln is unloaded, mark Fired. Members whose pieces were logged at loading see "ready to collect".

Notes
- Firings can be scheduled up to 8 weeks ahead and repeated weekly.
- Reservations close at the load time. Managers can add pieces after the close.
- Piece-level status ("loaded", "fired", "ready to collect") requires the manager to log pieces at loading; without that step members see only the firing status.
