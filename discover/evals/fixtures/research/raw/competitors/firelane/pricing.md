# Plans

Flat monthly price per studio, by member count.

## Studio — $79 / month
Up to 100 members.
- Firing schedule and email alerts
- Shelf reservations
- Firing log

## Guild — $149 / month
Up to 300 members.
- Everything in Studio
- Text (SMS) alerts before each firing
- Piece tracking: members see "loaded", "fired", "ready to collect"
- Two kilns

## Campus — $349 / month
Unlimited members, unlimited kilns, SSO. For departments and makerspaces.

All plans: 14-day trial, cancel any time. Annual billing takes 15% off.
