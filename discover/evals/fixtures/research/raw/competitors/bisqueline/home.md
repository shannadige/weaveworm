# Bisqueline

## Your studio, one calendar.
Wheel time, classes, and kiln firings on a single calendar your members already check.

For community ceramics studios and teaching studios with 20 to 300 members.

### What members get
- Book wheel time and open-studio hours from their phone
- See the firing calendar: every bisque and glaze firing for the month ahead
- Sign up for classes and pay online

### What studio owners get
- Membership billing with automatic renewals
- A firing calendar you set once and repeat weekly or monthly
- Attendance reports for classes and open studio

"We stopped printing the schedule. Members just look at Bisqueline." — studio owner, Portland
