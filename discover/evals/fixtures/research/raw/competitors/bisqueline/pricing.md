# Pricing

Priced per active member, billed monthly.

## Studio — $4 per member per month
Minimum $60 per month.
- Wheel-time and open-studio booking
- Firing calendar (repeating schedule)
- Membership billing and renewals
- Email reminders for classes

## Studio Plus — $6 per member per month
Minimum $120 per month.
- Everything in Studio
- Class sign-ups with waitlists
- Text reminders for classes and firings
- Attendance and revenue reports

## Enterprise — talk to us
For studios with multiple locations or more than 300 members.

FAQ
- What counts as an active member? Anyone with a current membership in the billing month.
- Can members see when their piece is fired? Members can see the firing calendar. Tracking individual pieces is on our roadmap.
- Is there a free trial? 30 days, no card needed.
