# Interview kit — Q-001 What causes members to miss the load deadline for a firing
<!-- weaveworm interview-kit v1 -->
Plan: Q-001 in research/plans.md

## Screener

| # | Question | Pass if |
|---|---|---|
| S1 | How many pieces have you put through a firing at the studio in the last two months? | 1 or more |
| S2 | Which studio do you fire at? | Northside Clay or Wheelhouse Collective |
| S3 | What do you do for work? | Not UX, design, or market research; not studio staff |

Disqualify: studio staff; works in UX, design, or market research; no piece fired in the last two months
Quota: 6–8 participants — members of the two partner studios, from the plan's Participants line

## Guide v1

Session length: 45 minutes

### Warm-up (8)
1. Tell me about the last piece you finished. How did it get from the wheel to fired?
   - Probe: Who else was involved along the way?

### Core (30)
2. Think about the last firing you wanted a piece in. How did you find out when it was happening?
   - Probe: Where were you when you found out?
   - Probe: What did you do next?
3. Tell me about a time a piece of yours missed a firing.
   - Probe: When did you realise? What happened to the piece?
4. How do you find out whether a piece has actually been fired?
   - Probe: What did you do the last time you weren't sure?
5. Would a countdown to the next firing in the app help you?

### Wrap (7)
6. Is there anything about firings at the studio I should have asked and didn't?
7. Is there another member you think I should talk to?
