- Renewal: "I'm being asked by the regional director whether this is worth $600 a month. Right now my answer is 'sort of'."
- What would help her: "If Nook just handled the nagging, I'd be happy. And if residents could tell me 'I'm away till the 14th' so I don't hassle them."
- Told me the 72-hour policy is the building's, not Nook's; Nook has no setting for it. "I put it in the welcome packet. Nobody reads the welcome packet."
