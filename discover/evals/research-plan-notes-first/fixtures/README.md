# Nook — parcel lockers for apartment buildings

Nook sells smart parcel lockers to property managers of mid-size apartment buildings (60–300 units). Couriers drop parcels into a locker; the resident gets an SMS/app notification with a pickup code. Buildings pay a monthly fee per bank of lockers. Product team: one PM (me), one designer, three engineers. ~140 buildings live, ~19,000 resident accounts.

Current problem as the CEO frames it: "lockers are full all the time, couriers can't deliver, building managers complain, and we're losing renewals."
