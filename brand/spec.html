Each skill is a worm. The worms wind the shell.

A visual system for the weaveworm plugin suite, drawn from Sekiguchi's cleanroom: a thin serif over pearl grounds, violet as the corporation's voice, and nothing rectangular but the page itself. Patterns are wound, images are grown, and every figure moves. The page demonstrates its own rules.

The worm appears only as mathematics

weaveworm is named for the weaveworms of Marathon — the organisms whose thread Sekiguchi winds into the biomatic shells runners wear. The name does the storytelling; the brand never illustrates it. No mascot, no worm drawings, no jokes in the copy. The worm survives as a line with an equation — and the colony above is the brand's source system: every image in this specification is those same worms, observed through a different instrument.

worm→thread→shell
skill→artifact→product

a skill spins one artifact; the artifacts interlace into the product. the metaphor and the architecture are the same diagram.

  1. 1

    One thread each

    Every pattern is a continuous path or an interlacing of continuous threads — several may work at once, but each is one unbroken line. Threads bend; they never break, and they never freehand. If a line can't state its equation, it doesn't ship.

  2. 2

    Violet marks the living

    Violet is how the corporation speaks: while work is under way it rides every moving tip; at rest it contracts to a single point. It arrives as points and lines, never as floods — its share is set in the color plate below.

  3. 3

    Nothing rectangular but the page

    Patterns, imagery and motion live entirely in curves — winding, waves, envelopes that breathe. The only right angles belong to typography, hairlines and the sheet itself. If a figure resolves into cells or pixels, it is rejected.

Pearl, chitin, and the Sekiguchi violet

The palette is the cleanroom: pearl grounds with a faint lilac cast, violet-black chitin ink, and the corporation's violet in place of any warning color. The violet here is calibrated by eye against the faction's in-game material — treat the hex as a fitting, not a fact, and correct it against captured reference. Marathon's acid green stays with the master brand. Light is canonical; dark values serve explicit dark mode only.

A thin serif leads; the mono specifies

The serif is Computer Modern — the typeface mathematics itself is set in, its hairline strokes drawn by Knuth's equations. For a brand whose every figure states its function, the association is not decoration; it is provenance. Overused Grotesk carries the argument; Departure Mono appears only where monospace is load-bearing. All faces are SIL OFL or free licenses, embedded outright — nothing from the Google or Adobe libraries, which also retired several handsome Didones from consideration.

Every stage owns a draft

Weaving notation is Boolean algebra: a drawdown is the product of threading, tie-up and treadling matrices. Each stage of the design stack is assigned a draft whose structure matches its work — the pattern system is an org chart, not wallpaper. Drafts render in the colony's own hand: thin laid threads that dive and surface, and on shipped stages a single shuttle — that stage's worm — still working the weft. Planned stages hold cloth no one is weaving yet.

Every image is a program, and every program moves

No photography, no stock, no gradients, no stills pretending to be figures. Imagery is the colony observed through different instruments: the cloth they have laid under wind, the body that moves behind it. It is rendered as thread and it moves the way silk moves — continuously, visibly, with periods you can feel. Every figure states its function and its motion law; an image that can't be regenerated doesn't exist.

motion law, all figures: continuous travel or precession, periods 4–20 s, visible at a glance · laid thread accumulates incrementally and is never redrawn, so each figure holds under ~2 ms per frame on ordinary hardware · violet rides what lives — every tip in motion, one point at rest — placed by the mathematics · animation pauses off-screen · static under reduced motion

  3. 3Every published figure carries its function and its motion law in the caption, the way a figure carries its source.
