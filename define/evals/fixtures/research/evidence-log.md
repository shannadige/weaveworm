## E-001 Analysts assemble the month-end close from exports out of three separate systems
- **Evidence:** 5 of 6 sessions opened with manual exports from the ERP, the bank feed, and the expense tool before any reconciliation began; P3: "Nothing talks to anything, so I go get it all myself."
- **Source:** usability sessions 2026-07 (P1–P6), close walkthrough guide v1
- **Question:** Q-001
- **Confidence:** high — observed behavior across 5 of 6 sessions

## E-002 Analysts discover stale bank data only after reconciliation is underway
- **Evidence:** P1, P3, and P4 each stopped mid-reconciliation to re-pull the bank export after finding balances that did not match; P4: "I was forty minutes in before I realized the feed was from Tuesday."
- **Source:** usability sessions 2026-07 (P1–P6)
- **Question:** Q-001
- **Confidence:** high — observed in 3 of 6 sessions
- **Stakes:** high — a stale feed reached a signed close in June and forced a restatement

## E-003 Analysts wait roughly half a day for a re-requested ERP export
- **Evidence:** P2 and P5 both described emailing the ERP owner and waiting "until after lunch" or "until the next morning"; no session observed the wait directly.
- **Source:** interviews 2026-07 (P1–P6), guide v2
- **Question:** Q-002
- **Confidence:** medium — self-reported by two participants

## ~~E-004 Analysts prefer email over the tool for reconciliation questions~~
- **Evidence:** 7 of 9 survey respondents chose "email" when asked where they raise reconciliation questions.
- **Source:** survey 2026-06 (R1–R9)
- **Question:** Q-002
- **Confidence:** medium — self-reported
- **Retracted:** the survey question listed only email and phone as options; the tool was not offered, so the result says nothing about preference.

## E-005 Controller review sends the close back to the analyst at least once in most months
- **Evidence:** P6 (controller) reported bouncing "most closes at least once"; P2 said "I plan for one round trip, sometimes two."
- **Source:** interviews 2026-07 (P1–P6), guide v2
- **Question:** Q-002
- **Confidence:** medium — self-reported by two participants from both sides of the review

## E-006 Analysts track which accounts are reconciled in a personal checklist outside the tool
- **Evidence:** P1, P2, P4, and P5 showed a spreadsheet or notes file they tick through; P2: "The tool doesn't know what's done, only I do."
- **Source:** usability sessions 2026-07 (P1–P6)
- **Question:** Q-001
- **Confidence:** high — observed in 4 of 6 sessions

## E-007 The ERP owner is unresponsive on close days
- **Evidence:** P5: "He's in his own close, so my export request sits."
- **Source:** interviews 2026-07 (P1–P6), guide v2
- **Question:** Q-002
- **Confidence:** low — single anecdote
