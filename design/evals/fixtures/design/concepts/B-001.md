# Concepts — B-001
<!-- weaveworm concept-sprint v1 -->
- **Brief:** B-001

## C-001 A currency readout at close kickoff
- **Mechanism:** When the analyst opens the month's close, the first thing they see is the three sources side by side, each with its last refresh and a plain verdict on whether it covers the period end. They read it, then pull their exports as they do today.
- **Serves:** J-001.1 shift, J-001.3 shift, OC-001
- **Trades:** Adds a stop at the top of every close, including the closes where nothing is stale, against the close-duration guardrail; the analyst still has to act on what they read.
- **Variants:** verdict shown on the close's landing screen; verdict delivered as a morning message on close days
- **Status:** chosen (→ D-001)

## C-002 Stale rows flagged inside the spreadsheet
- **Mechanism:** The analyst reconciles as today. As they reach an account whose source export predates the latest refresh, the row is marked stale in their own spreadsheet, and they decide there whether to re-pull.
- **Serves:** J-001.3 shift, OC-001
- **Trades:** Staleness still surfaces after reconciliation has started, so the "before they begin" half of OC-001 is only partly served; it also leans on the spreadsheet staying the surface, which the charter fences off as a Non-goal but does not promise to keep.
- **Status:** parked — surfaces staleness after work starts, and OC-001 asks for before

## C-003 The close cannot open until every source is confirmed current
- **Mechanism:** The analyst tries to open the close and is held at the door whenever a source is stale, with the time of the next refresh shown. Once every source covers the period end, the close opens and they proceed with no further checks.
- **Serves:** J-001.1 shift, OC-001
- **Trades:** With a nightly-only bank feed (given, bank integration contract) a stale feed costs a full day of close time, against the close-duration guardrail, and the analyst loses the judgment call on whether to proceed anyway.
- **Status:** parked — a hard gate against a nightly-only refresh costs a day per stale feed
